Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LOVERTORN RECORDS handles information when using the internal LOVERTORN publishing and catalog-management tool.

Information we process

  • Basic profile and account identifiers made available after a user authorizes a supported platform integration.
  • Public catalog and video metadata used to identify existing content and reduce duplicate uploads.
  • Media files and publishing metadata intentionally selected for upload or publication.
  • Operational logs needed for security, troubleshooting, auditability, and publishing integrity.

LOVERTORN does not request or store a user's TikTok password.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate authorized accounts, verify catalog state, prevent duplicate publishing, upload and publish authorized media, maintain operational security, and troubleshoot publishing workflows.

Sharing and sale of data

LOVERTORN RECORDS does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to the connected platform or technical service providers only as necessary to perform the requested integration or maintain the service.

Retention and security

Authentication credentials and tokens are retained only as needed to operate authorized integrations and are protected using local access controls and restricted operational processes. Operational records are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for security, troubleshooting, compliance, and audit purposes.

User control

Users can revoke platform access through the relevant platform's account or authorization settings. Revoking access prevents future API access using the revoked authorization.

Children

LOVERTORN is an internal business tool and is not directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when integration capabilities or operational practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to LOVERTORN RECORDS through its official social media channels or developer account contact methods.
